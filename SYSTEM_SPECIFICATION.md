# PCEA St Andrews Kindergarten
## Comprehensive School Management System Specification & Operational Guide
*Written in Clear, Everyday Language — Free of Technical Jargon*

---

### Purpose of this Document
This guide describes how the PCEA St Andrews Kindergarten Management System functions in plain everyday language. It is written for School Board Trustees, Headteachers, Deputy Principals, Class Teachers, Bursars, and School Administrative Staff. It explains who does what, how daily school tasks are handled, and how information moves securely through our school community.

---

## 1. Overview of the System
The PCEA St Andrews Kindergarten Management System is a centralized digital office for our school. It replaces paper filing cabinets, handwritten mark sheets, paper receipt booklets, and noticeboards with one secure, accessible place where teachers and administrators work together.

The system is organized around two primary roles:
1. **School Administrators (Headteachers, Principals & Bursars):** Oversee school admissions, approve fee records, manage staff and classroom schedules, and monitor school operations.
2. **Teachers (Class Instructors & Subject Teachers):** Input student test and exam marks and view and manage their class timetable.

---

## 2. Learner Admissions & Student Records
### What it does
Maintains a complete, permanent student card for every enrolled learner from the day they join the school until graduation or transfer.

### Information Kept for Each Student
- **Personal Details:** Full name, admission number, date of birth, gender, and current grade or classroom.
- **Family Contacts:** Parent or guardian names, verified telephone numbers, email addresses, and home residential address.
- **Health & Safety Details:** Dietary alerts, allergies, special educational needs, and emergency contacts.
- **Enrolment Status:** Active learner, officially transferred, or graduated alumnus.

### How Admissions Work (Step-by-Step)
1. **Application Received:** The school office enters the learner's details using the "Register New Learner" form.
2. **Admission Number Issued:** The system assigns an official student registration code that stays with the student throughout their school journey.
3. **Class Allocation:** The student is placed into their appropriate grade (e.g., Grade 10-A).
4. **Automatic Account Creation:** An electronic fee ledger and term report card are instantly created for the student.

---

## 3. School Fee Management & Receipts
### What it does
Provides the school bursar and parents with complete transparency over school fees, payment histories, and remaining balances.

### Key Capabilities
- **Term Fee Packages:** The school sets the standard fee structure per grade and academic term (including tuition, library resources, science laboratory access, sports activities, and meals).
- **Multiple Payment Channels:** Accommodates payments made via Bank Deposit, Mobile Money (e.g., M-Pesa), Direct Card Payment, or Bank Cashier.
- **Instant Official Receipts:** Each time a payment is logged, the system produces a numbered school receipt showing the amount paid, student details, date, remaining balance, and receiving officer.
- **Clear Arrears Monitoring:** At a glance, administrators can see total fees expected, total fees banked to date, and a list of students with outstanding balances.
- **Parent Fee Statements:** A one-page statement showing all payments and balance due can be printed or sent to parents at any time.

## 5. Academic Performance & Grading Standards
### What it does
Allows teachers to record class tests, mid-term examinations, and final term marks, automatically converting raw scores into standardized letter grades with meaningful remarks.

### Grading Scale Standard
- **Grade A (80% – 100%):** Excellent Mastery. Strong conceptual understanding and application.
- **Grade B (70% – 79%):** Very Good Performance. Consistently meets standards with minor gaps.
- **Grade C (60% – 69%):** Good Performance. Satisfactory understanding; needs targeted practice.
- **Grade D (50% – 59%):** Basic / Pass. Meets minimum requirements; requires extra study support.
- **Grade F (Below 50%):** Needs Urgent Improvement. Student requires teacher remediation and parent conference.

### Teacher Gradebook Workflow
1. Teacher selects their Class (e.g., Grade 10-A) and Subject (e.g., Mathematics).
2. Teacher chooses the assessment type (Quiz, Assignment, Mid-Term, or End-Term Exam).
3. The roster of enrolled learners loads automatically.
4. The teacher inputs the marks out of 100.
5. The system immediately calculates the letter grade, subject average, class high/low score, and suggests appropriate teacher remarks.

## 6. Student Term Report Cards
### What it does
Produces professional, comprehensive student academic reports for parents at the close of every school term.

### Contents of the Official Report Card
- **School Crest & Header:** Official school badge, term dates, and academic year.
- **Student Profile:** Full name, admission number, grade, and class teacher.
- **Academic Table:** All subjects listed with continuous assessment scores, final exam scores, total percentage, letter grade, and subject teacher comments.
- **Overall Performance:** Term average, class rank (e.g., 3rd out of 34 students), and total marks.
- **Pastoral & Conduct Feedback:** Class teacher's personalized behavioral summary and Principal's official closing remarks with signature block.
- **Print & Distribution:** Formatted to print cleanly on standard A4 stationery.

---

## 7. Master Timetable & Classroom Schedules
### What it does
Organizes the weekly flow of lessons across all grades and teaching staff, preventing double-booked classrooms or teachers scheduled in two rooms at once.

### Features
- **Weekly Grid:** Covers Monday through Friday across standard school periods (Periods 1 to 7, plus morning assembly, break time, and lunch).
- **Dual Perspective:**
  - **By Class:** Students and class teachers see their complete weekly schedule.
  - **By Teacher:** Individual teachers can view their personal teaching workload and free periods.
- **Double-Booking Shield:** If an administrator attempts to assign a teacher to two different classrooms during the exact same period, the system provides an immediate reminder to resolve the conflict.
- **Printable Timetables:** Easily printed for display on classroom wall boards and staff room noticeboards.

---

## 8. Security, Privacy & Safeguards
- **Clear Access Boundaries:** Teachers can only edit marks for their designated classes; financial ledgers and student admissions are reserved for administrative personnel.
- **Permanent Audit Record:** Important updates, fee entries, and grade submissions record the author and timestamp to maintain school integrity.
- **Parent Privacy:** Personal family contact information and sensitive health alerts are strictly kept confidential within the authorized school administration.
- **Continuous Backup:** All student files, fee records, and grades are preserved safely so that a computer malfunction in the staff room never results in lost school work.
